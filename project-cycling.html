Cycling Retail Sales Analysis

End-to-End Business Performance Evaluation & Tableau Visualizations

Project Overview

This project models transactional data frameworks and business workflows for a global cycling retail chain. Moving beyond raw record tracking, the objective was to design structural database integrity constraints and engineer comprehensive visual analytics to evaluate monthly revenue cycles, product category metrics, and organizational trends.

Core Business Metrics Evaluated

  • Sales & Revenue Operations: Identifying key seasonal revenue fluctuations and high-margin product categories.
  • Inventory Optimization: Monitoring structural stock and fulfillment data points across regional distribution nodes.
  • Customer Segments: Mapping purchasing trends and operational retention signals across demographics.

Technologies & Methodologies Used

  • Tableau Desktops: Designing interactive and dynamic executive-level metrics and dashboards for stakeholders.
  • Database Engineering & Joins: Structural parsing and link tracking across multiple sales tables to ensure financial validation.
  • Business Intelligence (BI) Dashboarding: Developing clean, user-centric visualizations for quick trend discovery.
