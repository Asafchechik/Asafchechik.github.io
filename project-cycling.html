Cycling Retail Performance Analysis

End-to-End Business Intelligence & Strategic Tableau Dashboards

Project Overview & Business Impact

This business intelligence architecture models complex transactional data frameworks and logistical workflows for a global cycling retail chain. Moving far beyond static reporting, this project designs structured database schemas and interactive analytical frameworks to track sales operational metrics, evaluate monthly revenue cycles, and identify inventory lifecycle trends. The final dashboards serve as an executive-level control room to support real-time resource allocations and improve regional supply chain resilience.

Core Business Operations Evaluated

  • Sales & Profitability Streams: Mapped and isolated hidden seasonal revenue fluctuations, identifying high-margin bike categories and product bundles to optimize marketing spend.
  • Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization: Monitored product stock distribution curves and fulfillment delay signals across multi-regional warehouse nodes to minimize inventory holding costs.
  • Customer Behavior & Demographics: Segmented transactional layers to identify high-value customer profiles, retention drops, and core purchasing trends over time.

Technologies & Analytical Methodologies

  • Tableau Desktop Architecture: Engineered comprehensive, interactive visual systems using calculated fields, parameter controls, and dynamic filtering for stakeholder review.
  • Relational Data Modeling & Joining: Conducted structural data schema definitions, cleaning, and normalization across disparate sales, inventory, and location tables to validate financial records.
  • Actionable Insight KPI Design: Formulated structured dashboard user interfaces to deliver lightning-fast data discovery and isolate underperforming assets at a single glance.
