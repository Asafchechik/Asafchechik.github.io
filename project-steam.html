Decoding Steam: Strategic Market Intelligence

Project Overview

Strategic audit of 6,417,106 user reviews from Steam, translating raw data into actionable Business Intelligence.

Key Insights

  • Top 5% of games generated 69.7% of reviews.
  • Positive reviews account for 82.3% of the dataset.
